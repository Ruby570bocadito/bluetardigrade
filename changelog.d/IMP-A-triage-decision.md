Alert triage now records a verdict: POST /api/alerts/{id}/status accepts `decision` (`false_positive`, `authorized_activity` or `confirmed_incident`) alongside the status, GET /api/alerts and the alerts export carry it (CSV appends the `decision` column at the end), and the noise report adds `false_positive_pct` — the real per-rule false-positive rate from recorded decisions, next to the existing `closed_pct`/`acknowledged_pct` proxies. Omitting the field in a later request clears the verdict (the body is the full triage record).
