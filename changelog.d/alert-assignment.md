- **Assign alerts from the engine (TEAM-3, backend).** The alert lifecycle gains the assignment trio — `assignee`, `assigned_by`, `assigned_at` — with two new routes: `POST /api/alerts/{id}/assign` (assign, reassign by assigning over, or unassign with the empty assignee) and `POST /api/alerts/assign-batch` (up to 500 ids from the console's selection bar, per-id `assigned`/`failed` result, deliberately not atomic). Assignment is WHO owns the triage, status is WHERE the triage sits: recognizing or closing never unassigns, and assigning never triages. `GET /api/alerts` gains `assignee=<name>` («Mis alertas», case-insensitive) and `unassigned=true` (the unassigned queue), applied after the lifecycle overlay so both backends (memory ring and SQLite) behave alike; the batch route verifies existence for every caller so a stale selection cannot create phantom assignments. Every change broadcasts the existing `alert_lifecycle` SSE frame — the console needs no new subscription. The OpenAPI spec documents the routes, the schemas and the window semantics of the store-mode filters. The console side («Mis alertas» view, selection-bar actions) is C-2 and follows this contract.
