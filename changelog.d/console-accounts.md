- **Accounts from the console.** Administrators manage the console accounts in Ajustes → Cuentas: create, change the role, disable or enable, reset the password, remove the two-step sign-in, delete. A new account or a reset password gets a temporary password, shown once; its owner must choose their own at the next sign-in, after the second step if any, before any session opens. Disabling an account, or changing its role or password, ends its sessions. An administrator cannot change their own account there, and no change may leave the console without an enabled administrator. Every action is audited with the account it targeted, and the audit panel shows that account.
- **Change your own password.** Anyone signed in with an account changes their password from the session panel (the current one, then the new one twice); their other sessions end.
- `sf-engine doctor` validates the new `disabled` and `must_change` fields and warns when no enabled administrator remains. Setting a password with `console-user.mjs --file` cancels a pending forced change.
