- **Backlog replay after an engine stop no longer turns the console upside down.** The ETW sensor runs as a Windows service and keeps capturing while the engine is stopped; on the next start it delivers its in-memory queue (up to 50,000 events) and disk spool at full speed, hundreds of events per second for minutes. The console applied every SSE event frame as its own update, so the live feed, the host process tree and the graphs re-rendered at that rate: SSE events are now merged in batches every 500 ms (alerts stay per frame). Live entity graphs (host, dashboard, NOC) redraw at most every 3 s and keep the nodes already on screen pinned where they were, so a destination entering or leaving the window no longer reshuffles the whole picture. The per-host risk score now charges each alert to its triggering event's time, like the correlator, beaconing and thresholds already do: a replayed backlog decays as it would have live instead of landing as one burst of fresh points (20 six-hour-old high alerts used to score ~105, "critical"). The sensor tile formerly labelled "En cola de disco" is now "Desbordados a disco": the sensor reports a cumulative counter since it started, not the backlog still pending.
