- **Host groups.** The engine groups machines by host-name pattern, Active Directory OU or ingest credential (`-host-groups <file.yaml>`; the Windows launcher always passes `tools\config\host-groups.yaml`). `GET /api/groups` lists the groups with their machines, and `PUT /api/settings/groups` saves them (needs `-api-write`; refused with 409 after a hand edit). In the console, administrators edit the groups in Ajustes → Grupos, and Equipos shows each machine's groups and filters by group. `sf-engine doctor` validates the file. Groups are the base of the per-account permissions that come next; nobody sees less because of them yet.
- **Active Directory settings through the console.** The console's proxy now forwards `PUT /api/settings/ad` and `POST /api/ad/test` for administrators. It refused them before, so the console could not save or test the connector unless the browser talked to the engine directly.
