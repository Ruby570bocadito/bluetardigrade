- **Shutdown can no longer be stretched by one slow notification.** The notify drain's 2s budget was only checked between frames: each delivery attempt ran under `context.Background()` with no ceiling, so the first queued frame could take its channel's full internal timeout — a wedged SMTP server (15s) turned a 2s graceful drain into a ~17s engine shutdown, with `nt.Wait()` blocking the whole stop sequence. The drain budget is now enforced per attempt: every delivery runs under a context that expires with the time the drain has left (the email channel applies `ctx.Deadline` to its socket and fails fast), the frame popped when the budget is spent counts as dropped without attempt, and the shutdown latency stays near the promised 2s regardless of queue depth.
