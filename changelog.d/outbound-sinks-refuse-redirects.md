- **Outbound alert sinks refuse redirects.** The SIEM webhook, the Slack and Telegram channels, the Elasticsearch and Splunk sinks and the reputation lookups all delivered through an `http.Client` with the default redirect policy: a receiver answering 3xx made the engine re-POST the alert payload — and, on a same-host redirect, the Bearer, ApiKey or HEC token with it — to whatever host the `Location` header named, including a downgrade to cleartext on the follow. Every one of these clients now hands the redirect back unanswered (`ErrUseLastResponse`, the same policy the rule webhook action, the doctor probes and the report delivery already used), so a 3xx counts as one definitive failed delivery in the existing counters and nothing ever leaves for the redirect target. The reputation client had the sharpest edge: its `x-apikey`/`Key` headers are custom, and Go does not strip custom headers on cross-host redirects.
